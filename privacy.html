2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Fill out our contact or booking form (name, email address, phone number, event type, and message)
  • Subscribe to our newsletter (email address)
  • Contact us directly via phone or email

We do not collect payment card details directly; any payment processing is handled by trusted third-party providers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and booking requests
  • Send you event confirmations and service-related communications
  • Send promotional emails about our services (only if you have opted in)
  • Improve our website and service offerings
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • EmailJS — our contact form delivery service, used to transmit form submissions to our inbox
  • Service vendors — trusted event vendors we may coordinate with on your behalf, only with your prior consent
  • Legal authorities — if required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. Booking and enquiry records are retained for up to 3 years after service delivery. Newsletter subscribers may unsubscribe at any time by contacting us at info@kmjcatering.com.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website does not use tracking cookies or analytics scripts. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar third-party tracking tools. The only external services loaded on our site are:

  • Tailwind CSS CDN (for styling)
  • Google Fonts (for typography)
  • Font Awesome CDN (for icons)
  • EmailJS (for contact form delivery)
  • Unsplash (for images)

These third-party services may collect IP addresses or usage data as part of their standard operation, subject to their own privacy policies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@kmjcatering.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our website is served over HTTPS. Contact form data is transmitted via EmailJS's encrypted service.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information about a child, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last modified" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
